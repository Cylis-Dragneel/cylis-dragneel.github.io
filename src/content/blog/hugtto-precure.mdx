## Introduction
Starting off I do have to preface this by saying this is my first ever mahou shoujo. I tried getting into Madoka once upon a time but gave up mid episode 1 I think because the faces felt off to me. I then also watched 2 episodes of Heartcatch like a month or two before this season and while I did like the start I had no motivation to continue. And I'll be frank, if this wasn't a contract I would've dropped it very early on... most notably when the bane of my existence Emiru gets introduced. If you have come from the discord message I think you already know my thoughts on the creature that is Emiru but more on her existence later. This was definitely the contract I had the most pain getting through 2nd after perfect blue (overrated film). I don't do well with bright colours, and especially I absolutely abhor sharp/high pitched voices as they just give me a headache. I physically couldn't watch more than 2-3 episodes of Hugtto in a row as I was in agony. So yea you can guess how my experience was like.

## Story
The story for the most part is a typical villain of the week scenario which brings us to the Oshimaidas.... fucking annoying pieces of shit. They're just pokemon (in the sense that they only say their name) and god did they make a normal phrase like "Oshimaida" a fucking trigger word for me in whichever anime I watch <Emoji name=":sob:" /> Other than that I do like the overarching message of "You can be anything. The future is limitless" I just could never fully get invested in it mainly because of the characters used to deliver this message and more on them later. The overarching plot of saving the future from the Criasu Corporation who froze time in the future then came to the past too to freeze time here and steal the white mirai crystal later turned mother mirai crystal or whatever... just not a compelling, like the entire ideology behind stopping time is flawed which I'll get to when talking about George Cry because he's stupid. The moments of weakness the various characters had with self doubt if they can even do something and then regaining the hope is a touching narrative but a bit overused imo like come on hana this is like the 5th time you have said "We can't do it" in the last half of the series only for the same outcome each time.

## Characters
Moving onto the characters well... I have way more negative things to say than positive so I'll get the positive out of the way first. First of all, Hana's family is goated <Emoji src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/1408087182257946735.webp?size=96&name=GOATED" alt="GOATED" /> like genuinely I liked all of her family memebers more than her <Emoji name=":sob:" /> grandma, dad, mom, and lil sis were all fun to watch and especially the parents were some of the good parents in anime which isn't a high bar but still an achievement. Then we have Ruru Amour, by far my favorite character in the series and ironically the only who comes close to her? Is her own father Doctor Traum <Emoji src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/998735492105388132.webp?size=96" alt="ICANT" /> I genuinely loved both of them a lot. For Ruru seeing her growth from a typical android who just follows orders to someone who learns to feel emotions, do things of her own free will, get a heart, learn to love other it was all just peak, perfection <Emoji src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/854942691703390238.webp?size=96" alt="chef's kiss" /> And then Doctor Traum, omg a villain that actually thinks. Man was so goated like ~60 cures had to team up to stop his shenanigans truly built different. The father daughter dynamic between him and Ruru was also so sweet to watch and it is one of the 2 times I cried (the 2nd or well 1st one was just in Ruru's own arc near the start-ish when Hana's mother accepts Ruru even after she tells them she's just a stranger). Homare I also actually liked practically throughout from start to end, just a solid character and I loved her struggles. And since we are on Homare Anri also deserves a mention or Cure Infini, however you want to call him <Emoji src="https://cdn.discordapp.com/emojis/998464222956621824.webp?size=96" alt="Garf" /> another solid arc, I loved his descent into depression and joining Criasu and then that leading to him becoming an honorary cure. Saaya I was mostly indifferent towards through most of the series, but seeing all of like the 5 birth sequences with her show grows to be more prominent then her final arc with her mother where she declares her newfound dream of being a doctor, it was a nice and sweet character progression. Harry is also ok, I didn't mind harry though his hamster form was annoying cause his voice would just get higher pitched. Ok that's enough for the positives, time for all the negatives aka every single goddamn "main" character left.

Holy fuck, **Emiru** is an annoying piece of shit like genuinely infuriating creature that exists and I dare not bring her up to the level of any other being in existence. I hate her character, I hate the way she talks, I hate every single goddamn thing about her. Her archetype of being overly cautious for no fucking reason at all, I hate those people irl too and putting someone like that in fiction is just unnecessay. People say that she grows on you, no she fucking doesn't. I don't CARE if her family is partly to blame for how she is like because I fucking HATE them too, genuinely praying on their downfall every goddamn episode. Her becoming a cure was the actual downfall of my entire experience with the show there is genuinely nothing that can make up for anything. She safely joins Rachel from Tower of God as my most hated characters in media. I won't go on and on about her more though as I have definitely expressed my grievances before too while watching but yea.

**George Cry** this man is a joke, like no actually ![George Cry saying umbrella's are offensive](/blog/img/george-cry-saying-umbrella's-are-offensive.png) not to mention his logic for stopping time is so stupid, like Ah yes there is so much suffering in the world we must not let the future come cause people will just suffer so we must stop time so that people can stay happy forever and not suffer... Meanwhile the people who are suffering when he stops time will technically then be suffering for eternity using his logic.... Yea he didn't think that one through at all. Also he's downright creepy, idk who it is we see in the flashback but I can only assume his wife/lover but stop seeing your wife in a child sir! I know he is meant to be a husk of a man, but he is just uninteresting and nonsensical I just couldn't care about him at all.

Ok rapid fire time since I can't spend paragraphs on each character, Hana is mediocre at best, her fure fures got tiring within like the first 5 eps and overall not the kind of character I care about (I have to repeat, voices do a lot of work in making me not care about a character). Pupple, charareet and all the other randoms that converted from Criasu to the side of good just meh. Pupple was at least decent ig but all the other characters were just quirky and I just dislike quirky characters in general. Especially that 5 minutes guy, fuck that guy in particular. Hugtan... do I need to say more other than she's an annoying af baby? I hate babies in general so having one this important to the story was just a pain

This section will be short as I don't have anything specific to say. The endings on the other hand I did like more and neither of them got boring, which might be in part due to them switching it midway-ish though I vastly prefer ED1 to ED2 and no it definitely does not have anything to do with Emiru being in the second one, what? ## Conclusion
Overall it was an ok show, one I had a lot of gripes with and as my intro to the mahjou shoujo genre... not too thrilled. And it's definitely a show I would've dropped and never looked back at if it weren't for contracts. However, this does not mean I am against trying any other precures... I am somewhat interested in Meitantei cause mystery is my favorite genre in media... Blossom did tell me to revisit Heartcatch one day and told me that I might like tropical rouge so I might decide between the 3 after contracts, spin a wheel, or blossom if you _really_ want me to pick one particular one of the 3 I could do that.
